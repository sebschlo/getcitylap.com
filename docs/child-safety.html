Citylap Safety

Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: July 16, 2026

The team behind Citylap has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Citylap is an adults-only service: you must be at least 18 to use it, and the app is not directed to children. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we remove it.

Reporting

If you become aware of child safety concerns involving Citylap, please report them to us by email. Child safety reports are prioritized.

info@technicalassemblage.com

How we respond

When we identify apparent CSAE, we remove the content, terminate the responsible account, and report apparent CSAM to the U.S. National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and other regional or national authorities as required by law.
